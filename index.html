01 The Club

Athena
Run Club

A running community for beginners and comeback runners. No pressure, no comparisons, no “I'm not built for this”. Just a clear plan, a coach who knows you, and a crew that runs with you.

i.

Start

From exactly where you are today, even if you've never run a single minute.

ii.

Confidence

Technique, breathing, pace and listening to your body. Every session builds trust in yourself.

iii.

Consistency

Not a one-month challenge. A habit that stays, with a crew that holds you on the hard days.

Free.always.

Joining Athena Run Club costs nothing. No fees, no subscriptions, no catch. Coach Esra set herself one mission: to help everyone train, move and be healthy. All you need to bring is yourself.

03 Meet your coach

Esra Kaya.
Founder & coach.

Personal trainer, run coach and the founder of Athena Run Club. She coaches every member personally and for free, because her goal is simple: to help everyone train and live healthier. This is her story, in her own words: it all started outside.

01/03
Chapter i.

The trails taught me to keep going

On a rocky peak in thick fog, on a forest path, at the mouth of a cave above a valley, I have always found myself in motion. Out there I learned something simple: you don't need to see the summit to start climbing. You just take the next step.

Chapter ii.

Your body is a home, not a project

Tennis at sunset, stretching under a tree, the gym on a Sunday morning. Sport has never been a punishment for me. It's how I feel alive, strong and present, and that's the energy I bring to every session.

Chapter iii.

Then Athena was born

Among ancient stones and seaside sunsets, the idea of a club was born. I named it after Athena, goddess of wisdom and strength, because good running needs both: the strength to start, and the wisdom to keep going.

09 Questions

Good to
know.

Still unsure? Send coach Esra a message, there are no silly questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. No membership fee, no subscription, no hidden costs. Coach Esra's goal is simply to help everyone move and live healthier.

I've never run before. Can I join?

Absolutely. Beginner sessions use the walk–run method and are designed for people starting from zero.

I'm slow. Will I hold the group back?

No. Everyone runs at their own pace and nobody gets left behind. That's the whole point of the club.

What should I bring?

Comfortable running shoes, clothes for the weather, water and a good mood.

How do I sign up for a run?

Choose a run in the schedule, tap “I'm in” and send us a message. Or write to @athena.runclub on Instagram.

What about my health?

If you have a chronic condition, a recent injury or are pregnant, talk to your doctor before you start and let the coach know.

What if the weather is bad?

Any change or cancellation is posted in the schedule and under “Upcoming activities” on this page, and on Instagram.
